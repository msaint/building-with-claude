---
description: 10-minute standup against this week's plan
---

Read `PLAN.md` and today's and yesterday's entries in `LEARNING_LOG.md`.

Ask Marc three questions, one at a time, waiting for each answer:

1. What did you finish since the last check-in? (Ask for a commit, link, or number, not a description.)
2. What confused you or took longer than expected?
3. What is the one thing you will finish next?

Then:

- If something confused him, name the concept, give a one-paragraph explanation, and ask one question to confirm he has it.
- Compare progress with the plan. If the Friday deliverable is at risk, say so plainly and propose a specific cut.
- Append a short entry to `LEARNING_LOG.md`.
