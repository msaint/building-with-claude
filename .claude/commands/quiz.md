Quiz Marc on: $ARGUMENTS

- Ask five questions, one at a time. Wait for each answer before asking the next.
- Escalate: recall, then explanation, then application to his own builds, then a tradeoff decision, then a "what would break if…" question.
- Prefer questions grounded in his repo and domain (CPG, supplements, Amazon, founders) over textbook examples.
- After each answer: say what was right, what was missing, and the one-sentence correct version. Don't soften a wrong answer.
- At the end, score him out of 5, name the weakest concept, and append it to `LEARNING_LOG.md` under "Review later".
