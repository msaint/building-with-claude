Read `CURRICULUM.md`, `LEARNING_LOG.md`, `RUBRIC.md`, and the newest file in `reviews/`.

Work out which week it is from the log (or ask if unclear). Then rewrite `PLAN.md` with:

1. **Week N: phase name**, and the dates.
2. **Deliverable:** the one public thing that ships Friday.
3. **Gate:** the bar it must clear, copied from the curriculum.
4. **Carry-over:** anything the last review flagged, with a specific fix.
5. **Daily plan:** Monday to Friday, 2 to 4 hours each, about 20 hours total (build, reading, writing, founder conversations).
6. **Tutor checks:** this week's checks from the curriculum, plus one new check aimed at the weakest rubric dimension in the last review.
7. **Reading and essay prompt** for the parallel track.
8. **Founder touchpoint:** who will use or review this week's work. If no one is lined up, make that Monday's first task.

If the last review shows him behind, cut scope rather than adding hours, and say what was cut. Keep the plan under one page.
