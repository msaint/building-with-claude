# Rubric

Score 1 to 5 on each dimension, from public work only. Reviews at the end of every week; a fresh-session examiner also scores at weeks 4, 8 and 12. A gap of 2+ points between self-score and examiner score is a flag.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical currency | Can follow a tutorial | Ships working tools with Claude | Explains agent, tool and eval design choices with his own measured data |
| Judgment | Builds what is possible | Builds what is useful | Knows what not to build, and why |
| Founder empathy | Has opinions | Has run sessions | Has a written record of what blocks founders and what fixed it |
| Communication | Long and hedged | Clear | A founder or researcher can act on it after one read |
| Mission literacy | Knows the talking points | Can explain the RSP and the constitution | Connects safety work to concrete product decisions |
