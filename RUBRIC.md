# Rubric

Score 1 to 5 on each dimension, from public work only. Reviews at the end of every week; a fresh-session examiner also scores at weeks 4, 8 and 12. A gap of 2+ points between self-score and examiner score is a flag.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical currency | Can follow a tutorial | Ships working prototypes with coding agents | Prototypes a new capability himself in a week and explains the design with his own measured data |
| Judgment | Builds what is possible | Builds what is useful | Writes kill criteria before building, follows them, and can show a bet he stopped |
| User evidence | Has opinions | Has watched people use it | Has a written record of what real users did, what it changed, and who came back |
| Communication | Long and hedged | Clear | A researcher or an executive can act on it after one read |
| Research and mission literacy | Knows the talking points | Can explain a recent paper, the RSP and the constitution | Connects a research result, and its risks, to a concrete product decision |

The anchors above were rewritten for the Research PM, Labs target on 10 Nov 2026 (week 4). Weeks 1 and 2 were scored against the earlier Safeguards anchors. In `progress/weeks.json` the third and fifth dimensions keep their original keys, `founder_empathy` and `mission`.
